Public Welfare Dilemma

Bridging the Gap Between Citizens & Public Schemes

Tens of thousands of crores in public assistance remain unclaimed every year simply because the right citizens never know they qualify.

❌

THE PROBLEM

People often miss out on crucial public benefits because:

  • ✗ They don't know which schemes exist across departments
  • ✗ Eligibility guidelines are confusing and dense
  • ✗ Information is scattered across hundreds of portals
  • ✗ Application procedures are difficult to navigate
  • ✗ Required documents and formats are unclear
  • ✗ Important government deadlines are quietly missed
✅

THE SOLUTION

ClaimIt transforms welfare discovery with automated guidance:

  • ✓ Personalized discovery based on your profile
  • ✓ Rule-based multi-criteria eligibility matching
  • ✓ Simple, transparent 'Why you qualify' explanations
  • ✓ Interactive document readiness checklist
  • ✓ Step-by-step application guidance roadmap
  • ✓ Direct official application links & status tracking
